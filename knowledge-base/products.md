# What packaging formats does Spectalpack offer?

- Spectalpack provides a full range of digital flexible-packaging products. Our portfolio includes:

# Stand-up Pouch:

Custom stand-up pouches are designed to capture attention and enhance product visibility on the shelf, whether in a retail store or at a farmer’s market. Suitable for all kind of products.

# Flat Bottom Pouch & Quad seal pouches:

Flat bottom pouches, also known as 3D / box pouches, are a premium packaging solution designed for both stability and visibility. It’s front panels allow for excellent product visibility and side-gussets give it the option of displaying additional information or even a window. These pouches are best suitable for premium products.

# Pillow Pouch/ Centre Seal Pouches:

Center seal, or pillow pouch packaging, is a popular flexible packaging solution known for its simple yet effective design.Ideal for snacks, masalas, powders, and other lightweight products

# Center Sealed Sachet Pouch:

Custom center sealed sachet pouches are designed to capture attention and enhance product visibility on the shelf, whether in a retail store or at a farmer’s market. Suitable for all kind of products.

# Lay-flat/ 3-side Seal Pouches:

A 3-side seal pouch is a type of flexible packaging that is sealed on three sides, with one side left open for filling. These pouches are ideal for single serve uses or to be used as sachets.

# Roll Stock:

Rollstock offers incredibly fast turnaround with reduced energy use and waste compared to traditional printing methods. It runs seamlessly on vertical and horizontal FFS equipment, as well as on manual and automated machinery.

# Shrink Sleeves:

Shrink sleeves are a type of packaging label that conforms tightly to the shape of a container when heat is applied. These sleeves offer 360-degree coverage, allowing for vibrant, full-color graphics and branding. Shrink sleeves are widely used for bottles, cans, and jars, providing phenomenal aesthetic appeal

- These formats cover virtually every common need in flexible packaging. We can also produce specialized options (zipper pouches, spout pouches.) on request.

# Security Printing

We offer cutting-edge HP Digital Security Printing solutions to protect your brand and products from counterfeiting. Our advanced technology allows us to print multiple security elements in a single pass on one press, providing layered protection from overt to forensic levels. Our partnership with industry leaders ensures we can offer the most comprehensive protection for your packaging, safeguarding your brand integrity and instilling confidence in your customers.

# Overt Features

Implement easily visible security elements like QR codes, microtext, and guilloche patterns for instant verification

# Covert Elements

Incorporate hidden images, digital fiber patterns, and anti-copy codes for enhanced protection against counterfeiting.

# UV-Reactive Inks

Utilize invisible yellow/blue inks that are only visible under ultraviolet light for discreet authentication.

# Taggant Inks

Apply forensic-level security with Sherlox and GOSure taggant inks for irrefutable product verification.

# Variable Secure Designs

Create unique, differentiated security designs for each package, making replication extremely difficult.

# Track & Trace Solutions

Implement serialization and smart codes for individual product tracking throughout the supply chain.
